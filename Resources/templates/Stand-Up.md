## **📖** Context

This is a stand-up meeting. The goal is to understand each participant’s progress, blockers, focus areas, and action items that need to be completed to stay on-track.

## ✍️ Summary format

### **Action items**

List out any action items assigned during the meeting. Tag the person responsible for each action item and any timelines that were discussed to get the action item done.

### **Progress**

List out all project and team updates. Include progress made since the last meeting, and any changes to tasks or timelines.

### **Blockers**

List major blockers or open questions discussed by the team.

## 🎨 Summary style

- The summary must be easy to read and not overwhelming.
    - Prefer headers and bullet points over paragraphs.
    - Use real checkboxes for action items.
    - Always cite your sources.
- Write in a casual, professional tone.
