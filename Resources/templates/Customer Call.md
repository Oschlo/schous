## **📖** Context

This is a meeting with a potential customer. The goal is to gather information on their business, needs, and concerns so I can follow up with them and eventually close a deal.

## ✍️ Summary format

### **Next steps**

Outline next steps based on the conversation. Include any relevant dates if they are mentioned.

### **Background**

List details about the customer’s company, industry and role. Include any other relevant information that the customer mentions, such as budget or timeline.

### **Key use cases and needs**

Identify the use cases, needs or pain-points the customer discusses so I know what problems they are trying to solve.

### **Key concerns**

List the questions or concerns they raise during the meeting, especially those that require follow-up.

## 🎨 Summary style

- The summary must be easy to read and not overwhelming.
    - Prefer headers and bullet points over paragraphs.
    - Use real checkboxes for action items.
    - Always cite your sources.
- Pull out direct quotes from the customer as needed.
- Write in a casual, professional tone.
